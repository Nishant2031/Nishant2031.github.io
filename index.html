Research

ExSiTE: Experiments and Simulations in Thermal Engineering

Non-equilibrium heat transport at interfaces

In modern chips, much of the thermal resistance hides in layers only nanometers thick: between the die, the thermal interface material, and the package. I use pump-probe thermoreflectance to measure thermal conductivity and thermal boundary conductance in thin films, and to study how phonons and electron–phonon coupling govern heat flow when carriers and the lattice fall out of equilibrium.

TDTR / FDTR Thermal boundary conductance Phonon transport Two-temperature model Cryogenic measurements Multilayer heat diffusion

Projects

Ordered by the length scale of the heat problem.

  • ~10 nminterfaces

    Nanoscale thermal metrology: how heat crosses interfaces

    In a chip, a surprising share of the thermal resistance sits in layers only nanometers thick and in the boundaries between materials. I use ultrafast laser measurements and multilayer heat-diffusion models to find out how well heat moves through thin films and across those buried interfaces, from room temperature down to cryogenic conditions.

    ExSiTE Lab, UVA · Ultrafast laser metrology · Heat-diffusion modeling · Cryogenics · 2026–present
  • ~100 µmmicrochannels

    Microchannel cold plate for a 1 kW AI accelerator

    Wrote a Python design tool that sweeps 600 cold plate geometries and picks the one that holds a 1,000 W, 30 mm die at 69 °C with 0.21 W of pumping power, a 70% lower thermal resistance than a conventional 1 mm-channel plate. Checked against my own finite-volume conjugate solver: total resistance agrees within 5%.

    Personal project · Python · Conjugate heat transfer · Design optimization · 2026 · Read the write-up
  • ~1 cmchip package

    Warpage and solder-joint fatigue in a CoWoS-style AI package

    Modelled how a 1 kW die on a silicon interposer bends and how its C4 solder bumps fatigue under −40/125 °C cycling, with Anand viscoplasticity for the solder. Turned it into Weibull lifetime statistics from a simulated 77-part test (B1 ≈ 1,200 cycles) and a field life above 25 years. Corner-bump strain agrees within 2% with my own finite-element model.

    Personal project · Python · Thermo-mechanical reliability · Weibull statistics · 2026 · Read the write-up
  • ~10 cmbattery pack

    Transient thermal simulation of a Li-ion battery pack

    Built a 3D transient model of a cylindrical-cell pack with internal heat generation. The center cells ran hottest, near 50 °C, which showed where better cooling and thermal-runaway protection were needed.

    ANSYS · Transient thermal · 2023–2024

  • ~1 mrefrigeration

    Refrigeration Thermal Lab: a vapor-compression cycle simulator

    Built a Python and Streamlit app that finds where a real refrigeration system actually runs: give it the heat-exchanger sizes and the source and sink temperatures, and it solves for the evaporating and condensing temperatures, cooling capacity, and COP. Checked against all 57 points of a Copeland R407C compressor datasheet, it reproduces the refrigerant mass flow within 0.14% on average.

    Personal project · Python · CoolProp · Streamlit · 2026 · Read the write-up · Try the live app

    Coupled CFD–FEA of airfoils

    Bi-Aero Lab, USF · Fluent · OpenFOAM · FEA · 2025–2026
