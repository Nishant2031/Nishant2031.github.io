5-DOF Robotic Welding Arm

2025–2026Kinematics, Mechanical Design, Embedded Control

Moving through the joint range.

Manual welding is slow, hot and hard to repeat, so for our senior design capstone I led a team of six to build an arm that does it for you. It is a 5-DOF arm, fully 3D-printed in PLA, driven by 12 V serial-bus servos and controlled from a Raspberry Pi, with a swappable end-effector that holds a welding torch or, for testing, a marker. We worked out the forward and inverse kinematics, checked deflection and tolerances, and then made it trace the same paths again and again. On straight lines it stayed within 0.22 cm of the target path on average.
